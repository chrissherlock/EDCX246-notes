1. The "Big 8" Theorists (Must Know)

Pierre Bourdieu
Cultural Capital & Habitus

Schools aren't meritocracies. They mistake middle-class habits (language, tastes) for natural intelligence. This inflicts symbolic violence on working-class kids.

Michel Foucault
Disciplinary Power & Panopticon

Schools use invisible surveillance (timetables, exams) to make students internalize rules. Creates docile bodies that self-police without physical force.

Ball & Lingard
Performativity & Datafication

Neoliberalism replaces trust with data (NAPLAN). Forces teachers into performativity (teaching to the test) and allows state governance by numbers.

Raewyn Connell
Hegemonic Masculinity

Schools are active gender regimes. The dominant, aggressive, stoic male ideal subordinates girls and marginalizes non-conforming boys.

Paulo Freire
Banking Model vs Praxis

Bad teaching = depositing facts into passive kids. Good teaching = problem-posing dialogue that creates conscientisation (critical awareness) and liberation.

Martin Nakata
The Cultural Interface

The complex space where Indigenous and Western knowledge meet. Indigenous students must be taught to master Western academic codes without losing cultural sovereignty.

Young & Delpit
Powerful Knowledge (Social Realism)

The progressive trap: Refusing to explicitly teach working-class kids standard grammar/science traps them in poverty. Schools MUST teach the codes of power.

UNCRC / Gillett-Swan
The 3 Ps & Tokenism

Provision, Protection, Participation (Art. 12). Schools protect kids but deny them real participation, reducing student voice to tokenism (e.g., picking prom colors).

2. Key Distinctions (X vs Y)

Concept A Concept B The Exam Difference
Biological Essentialism Social Construction Society makes categories (Gender, Childhood, Race), not biology. E.g., Brains are neuroplastic; male/female brain wiring is a "neuro-myth".
Medicalisation of Deviance Neurodiversity Paradigm Medicalisation (Conrad) turns bad behavior into sickness (ADHD) to drug kids instead of fixing schools. Neurodiversity (Singer) says brain differences are natural; fix the environment via UDL.
Hypodermic Needle Theory Active Audience (Hall) Media doesn't brainwash passive kids (hypodermic). Kids actively decode and resist polysemic messages based on their class/culture.
Technological Determinism Surveillance Capitalism Tech doesn't naturally fix education. EdTech platforms (Zuboff) harvest student data for profit and enclose public schools.
Deficit Discourse Institutional Racism Don't blame the student's culture/family for failing (e.g., the "Indigenous Problem"). Blame the biased, white-normative school policies and curriculum.

3. Ultimate MC/TF Elimination Rules

  • ❌ If an answer says schools are neutral, objective, or meritocratic → IT IS WRONG.
  • ❌ If an answer blames the student's biology, natural ability, or lack of effort → IT IS WRONG.
  • ❌ If an answer says standardized testing (NAPLAN) measures true learning → IT IS WRONG.
  • ✅ Look for answers mentioning structural inequality, cultural capital, or institutional power.
